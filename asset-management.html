Asset risk process

Each asset type has one shared assessment. A Risk Officer assesses hazards, risk ratings, controls, treatments and reviews through VERO’s existing Risk Management process. Every asset assigned to the type inherits that assessment.

Review on the scheduled date and after incidents, changes to equipment or use, new hazards or failed controls. Service records do not verify risk controls automatically.
